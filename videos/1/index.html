1. Pointy Half-Shell

Type: Back (Cloak)

Source: Ground Pickup

Location: Vennorax Wreckage

Coordinates:

Object: Spiked Shell

Travel south from the Shadowy Encampment to Vennorax Wreckage. Look under large animal bones on the left side of the wreckage for the glowing Spiked Shell object. Click to loot.

2. Soltian Wrangler's Grip

Type: Fist Weapon

Source: Kill & Loot

Location: Giant mushroom tops (various)

NPC: Squirming Mollusks (ID: 659300)

Use Bouncing Mushrooms, Air Spores, or Grappling Hook to scale the giant mushrooms. Kill Squirming Mollusks on top for a chance at this fist weapon transmog.

The video by 阿落 only showed coordinates but didn't mention the NPC is on TOP of the mushrooms. Many players wasted time searching the ground.

3–6. Ancient Crypt Reliquary

Type: Chest (4+ weapons)

Location: Vilaldoun Crypt

Coordinates:

Object: Ancient Crypt Reliquary (ID: 658802)

Enter Vilaldoun Crypt and explore all branching tunnels. The chest does NOT appear on the minimap — visually search every corner of the deepest chambers.
