🐾 Pet #1 — Patch (Wheelchair Cat)

A white cat in a wheelchair, a loving tribute to the Hearthstone classic character. Obtained through the anniversary hidden puzzle quest.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Go to Caverns of Time
    Travel to the Caverns of Time in Tanaris.
  2. Find Chromie and Get the Bloody Broom
    Inside the Caverns, find Chromie. Look for scattered parts on the ground and click them to receive the Bloody Broom.
  3. Use the Broom to Track Parts
    Equip or hold the Bloody Broom. Yellow dots will appear on your minimap indicating nearby collectible parts.
  4. Collect All 5 Parts
    Parts may be found in areas such as: Goldshire (Elwynn Forest), Razor Hill (Durotar), Crossroads (The Barrens), Dalaran (Broken Isles), Stormwind or Orgrimmar.
  5. Return to Chromie and Assemble Patch
    After collecting all 5 parts, return to the Caverns of Time and speak with Chromie. Complete the assembly and you will receive the achievement "Patches the Player". Patch the pet will be mailed to you!

🐐 Pet #2 — Baa'l (Little Baa'l Wawu)

A secret demonic goat battle pet, returning after 5 years. Originally from Battle for Azeroth, Baa'l is one of the most challenging secret pets to obtain in WoW history.

Returning in Patch 12.07

  1. Find the Starting NPC
    Travel to Frostfire Ridge (Warlords of Draenor) and locate the non-interactive NPC Marrow.
  2. Purchase the Detection Item
    Go to Vol'dun (Battle for Azeroth) and buy a special item from Lok'rath that allows you to see invisible demonic runes.
  3. Find the Hidden Runes
    Use the item in Darkshore (Cataclysm version) to reveal invisible demonic runes. Follow the rune trail.
  4. Travel to Blasted Lands
    Go to the Cataclysm version of Blasted Lands and find the invisible Baa'l NPC. Defeat it to unlock the ability to purchase the pet.
  5. Obtain Baa'l
    After defeating the invisible Baa'l, you can now purchase the Baa'l battle pet.

💡 Tips & Troubleshooting

  • Patch is tied to the Hearthstone Anniversary event — make sure the event is active before attempting.
  • The Bloody Broom is essential — without it, the yellow dots on your minimap will not appear.
  • Baa'l requires access to multiple expansion zones (WoD, BfA, Cataclysm). Ensure your character meets the level requirements.
  • Both pets can be obtained solo — no group required.
  • If a part isn't spawning, try relogging or /reload to reset phasing.
