Dreaming Salamander Aquatic Form Unlock Guide

Dragonflight · Druid Aquatic Form · True Fragrance Handbook Ep.131

🎯 Quick Summary

Map Valdrakken
NPC 陸佳 (Lu Jia)
NPC Coordinates /way 52.6 53.8
Fishing Trainer /way 35.0 48.6 (Sabu)
Method Fishing + Raid Scenarios
Required Fishing Rod, Fishing skill
Items to Collect Sleeping Moonfish x10 + Awakened Sunfish x10
Reward Dreaming Salamander 🐠

⚙️ Preparation

  1. Learn Fishing
    Go to Valdrakken and find Fishing Trainer Sabu to learn Dragon Isles Fishing.
    /way 35.0 48.6 (Sabu - Fishing Trainer)
  2. Find NPC Lu Jia
    Return to Valdrakken and locate the Raid Finder NPC Lu Jia.
    /way 52.6 53.8

🌟 Phase 1 — Sleeping Moonfish x10

  1. Enter the Scenario
    Talk to Lu Jia and select the 4th option from the bottom — "化身魚波" (Avatar of Fish Wave) to enter the raid scenario.
  2. Fish the Pool
    After entering, walk straight ahead to the pool of water. Use fishing to catch 10 Sleeping Moonfish.
  3. Leave the Instance
    Click your character portrait (top-left) and select "Leave Instance Group".

🔥 Phase 2 — Awakened Sunfish x10

  1. Enter the Second Scenario
    Talk to Lu Jia again and select the 3rd option from the bottom — "融火侵攻" (Molten Incursion) to enter the raid.
  2. Navigate to the Lava Ring
    Wait for the NPC to finish talking, then the left door will open. Ignore trash mobs and go straight to the boss arena's circular lava ring.
  3. Fish at the Lava
    Use fishing to catch 10 Awakened Sunfish from the lava.
  4. Leave the Instance
    Click your character portrait and leave the instance.

🎉 Phase 3 — Unlock the Appearance

  1. Return to the First Scenario
    Talk to Lu Jia again → "化身魚波" → re-enter the first scenario.
  2. Throw All Fish into the Pool
    Walk to the pool and throw all 20 fish (10 Moonfish + 10 Sunfish) into the water.
  3. Obtain the Synesthesia Angler Buff
    You will receive the "Synesthesia Angler" buff (3 minutes duration) in the top-right corner.
  4. Fish Up Qina
    Use fishing in the pool to catch "Qina" (Soulbound item).
  5. Use the Item
    Right-click Qina in your inventory → Appearance unlocked!

✂️ Changing Appearance

  1. Visit the Barber Shop
    Go to any Barber Shop in a major city (e.g. Valdrakken).
  2. Select Aquatic Form
    Navigate to Aquatic Form → select "Dreaming Salamander".

💡 Tips

  • You can fish in any form — no need to be in caster form.
  • The Synesthesia Angler buff only lasts 3 minutes — make sure to fish immediately after throwing the fish in.
  • If you fail, simply leave and re-enter the scenario to try again.
  • No fishing skill level requirement for this quest — any level works.
  • This is a Druid-only appearance unlock.
