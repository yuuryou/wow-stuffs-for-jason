Prerequisites

  1. Translation Gear (Recommended)
    Have "Translation - Mek-ghoulish" or similar translation items equipped to read hidden clues.
  2. Check Weekly Rotation
    Some steps require specific weekly rotation. If the chest is missing, wait for the next reset.

Step 1 — Find the Waterlogged Note

Go to the northwest coastline of Isle of Dorn. Search for shipwrecks and underwater debris to find the Waterlogged Note. Read it with translation gear equipped to reveal the hidden clues leading to the next location.

Step 2 — Follow Hidden Clues

Collect additional notes scattered across the island. Decode Morse code patterns and environmental symbols. Pay attention to references to "Deep" and "Cat" — these guide you toward an underwater cave. Use /sleep or other emotes if prompted by the clues.

Step 3 — Obtain Placid Deep Cat

Navigate the hidden underwater cave. You may need a buff that reveals invisible paths. In the deepest chamber, find the hidden treasure chest and open it to receive the Placid Deep Cat pet.

Tips

  • Chest not appearing? Try after the next weekly reset. Some steps rotate weekly.
  • Notes are gibberish? Equip translation gear to decode them.
  • This is a reskin of the Mists of Pandaria Fire Cat pet.
  • No combat — pure exploration puzzle.
