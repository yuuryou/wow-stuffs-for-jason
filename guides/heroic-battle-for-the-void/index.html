Overview

Complete the Heroic: Battle for the Void meta-achievement — consisting of 6 sub-achievements across the Val and Nalgathar zones — to earn the Tormented Devourer mount by exchanging 15 Void Radiance.

Patch: 12.07 The Darkest Night · Difficulty: Heroic · Estimated time: Several days to weeks depending on spawn cycles

Key Tips

1. Currency Exchange

After completing all 6 sub-achievements, collect 15 Void Radiance (虚光辉颜) and exchange them for the Tormented Devourer mount. Void Radiance drops from activities in Val and Nalgathar.

2. Weather Anomaly Timing

The Storm Response (Val) and Sporestorm (Nalgathar) quests are tied to zone weather events. Monitor zone conditions and plan your sessions around active weather anomalies.

3. Rare Spawn Tracking

For "Heroic Heavy Hitter", you need 15 rare kills. Use addons like RareScanner or SilverDragon to track spawns. Group up with other players for faster completion.

4. Commander Bosses

Node Captain Leixel and Premier Hotinax are elite bosses. They may require a small group to defeat. Check group finder for organized runs.

5. Affix Mobs

For "Energy Overflow", look for mobs with special glowing buffs. These are identified by unique visual effects on the mob. The exact number required may vary.
