1. Pointy Half-Shell

Type: Back (Cloak)

Acquisition: Ground Pickup

Location: Venorax Wreckage

Coordinates:

Object: Spiky Shell

Head south from the Dread Camp toward the Venorax Wreckage area. Look for a glowing Spiky Shell object beneath the large animal bones on the left side of the wreckage. Click to pick it up.

2. Soltian Wrangler's Grip

Type: Fist Weapon

Acquisition: Kill Loot

Location: Giant Mushroom Tops across Naigtal

NPC: Shambling Ooze

Use bounce mushrooms, air spores, or grappling hooks to climb to the top of giant mushrooms. Killing Shambling Oozes on top has a chance to drop the fist weapon transmog.

Video author A'Luo only gave coordinates but didn't mention the mushroom tops. Several NGA players reported searching on the ground for a long time before realizing they needed to be on the mushrooms.

3–6. Ancient Crypt Reliquary

Type: Chest (contains 4+ weapons)

Location: Verladon Crypt

Coordinates:

Object: Ancient Crypt Reliquary

Enter Verladon Crypt and carefully explore all branching passages. The chest does not appear on the minimap; you must visually search every corner of the deepest chambers.
