Prerequisites

  1. Arrive at Isle of Dorn
    Travel to the Isle of Dorn zone. This is the starting area for the secret puzzle.
  2. Translation Gear (Recommended)
    It is highly recommended to have translation gear such as "Translation - Mek-ghoulish" or similar items that allow you to read cryptic notes scattered across the island.
  3. Check Weekly Rotation
    Some decryption steps may be tied to weekly rotation. If you cannot find the chest, you may need to wait for the next weekly reset.

Step 1 — Find the Waterlogged Note

  1. Search the Northwest Coast
    Head to the northwest coastline of Isle of Dorn. Look for shipwrecks or underwater areas near the shore.
  2. Locate the Waterlogged Note
    Find the Waterlogged Note item. It may spawn in multiple locations near the water — check wreckage debris thoroughly.
  3. Read the Note
    Right-click the note to read it. If the text appears as gibberish, equip your translation gear and try again — the hidden message will be revealed.
Tip: If the Waterlogged Note is not visible, try switching shards or relogging. Some players report the note may be phased.

Step 2 — Follow the Hidden Clues

  1. Collect Scattered Notes
    The Waterlogged Note will give you clues to find additional notes scattered across Isle of Dorn. Follow the hints carefully — some notes are hidden under environmental objects.
  2. Decode Morse Code / Symbols
    Some clues involve Morse code patterns or symbols in the environment. Pay attention to unusual markings on rocks, trees, or structures. Use /sleep or other emotes if prompted.
  3. Look for "Deep" and "Cat" References
    The puzzle themes revolve around "Deep" and "Cat" — expect to head toward underwater caves or deep underground areas on the island.

Step 3 — Obtain the Placid Deep Cat

  1. Navigate the Underwater Cave
    After solving the clues, make your way to a hidden underwater cave on Isle of Dorn. You may need a temporary buff that reveals invisible paths.
  2. Find the Hidden Chest
    In the deepest part of the cave, locate a hidden treasure chest or interact with a concealed NPC.
  3. Loot the Pet
    Open the chest to receive the Placid Deep Cat pet. The item will be automatically added to your pet collection.

Tips & Troubleshooting

  • Chest not appearing? Some decryption steps may be tied to weekly rotation. If the chest is missing, try again after the next weekly reset.
  • Can't read the notes? Make sure you have equipped translation gear (such as Translation - Mek-ghoulish). Without it, the notes will appear as scrambled text.
  • This pet is a reskin of the Fire Cat pet from the Mists of Pandaria era — same model, different color scheme.
  • No combat required — this is purely a puzzle/exploration activity.
  • Switching shards can sometimes help if a note or chest is missing in your current phase.
