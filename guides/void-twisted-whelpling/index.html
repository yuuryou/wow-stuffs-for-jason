Overview

The Void-Twisted Whelpling is a rare mount obtained exclusively through fishing in the Voidstorm zone. Head to coordinates (50, 30) with your fishing rod equipped — no combat required, just patience and luck.

Warning: The water in this area may kill your character on contact. Some players reported dying simply by stepping into the water. Proceed with caution.

2. Preparation

Community tips from the video comments suggest preparing Perception Potions and food buffs to improve your fishing efficiency. One player mentioned crafting 40 perception potions for this grind.

3. Recommended Addon — Better Fisher

Use the Better Fisher addon for one-click fishing and amplified fishing sound effects. This makes the grinding process significantly more comfortable, especially for long sessions.

4. Drop Rate & Difficulty

The drop rate appears to be low and RNG-dependent. Comments indicate:

  • Some players got it within 5 minutes
  • Others reported needing 3 days of continuous fishing
  • One player mentioned fishing from midnight until morning before it dropped
Bottom line: Your mileage may vary significantly. Don't get discouraged!

5. ⚠ Water Hazard

Multiple players in the comments reported that stepping into the water in this area results in instant death. The author confirmed this with a humorous reply: "I knew there'd be some rebellious little cuties trying it." Avoid walking into the water — fish from the shore.
