🐾 Pet #1 — Patch (Wheelchair Cat)

A white cat in a wheelchair, a loving tribute to the classic Hearthstone character "Patches". This pet is obtained through the Hearthstone Anniversary hidden puzzle quest, earning you the achievement "Patches the Player" (全能帕奇！).

How to Obtain

  1. Travel to Caverns of Time
    Go to the Caverns of Time in Tanaris and find Chromie.
  2. Obtain the Bloody Broom
    Look for scattered parts on the ground near Chromie and click them. You will receive the Bloody Broom (染血的掃帚).
  3. Track Parts with the Broom
    Equip or hold the Bloody Broom — yellow dots will appear on your minimap indicating nearby collectible parts.
  4. Collect All 5 Parts
    The 5 parts are scattered across Azeroth. Common locations include:
    • Goldshire, Elwynn Forest
    • Razor Hill, Durotar
    • Crossroads, The Barrens
    • Dalaran, Broken Isles
    • Stormwind City / Orgrimmar
  5. Assemble Patch and Claim Your Pet
    Return to Chromie at the Caverns of Time. Complete the assembly quest and earn the achievement "Patches the Player". Patch will be mailed to you!

🐐 Pet #2 — Baa'l (Little Baa'l Wawu)

Baa'l is a secret demonic goat battle pet that first appeared in Battle for Azeroth. After a 5-year wait, Baa'l returns in Patch 12.07 with a new way to obtain this elusive companion. Originally one of the most complex secret hunts in WoW history, the 12.07 version streamlines some steps while preserving the mystery.

How to Obtain (12.07 Version)

  1. Step 1: Frostfire Ridge — Find Marrow
    Travel to Frostfire Ridge (Warlords of Draenor) and locate the non-interactive NPC Marrow near Thunderlord Stronghold.
  2. Step 2: Vol'dun — Buy the Rune Detector
    Travel to Vol'dun (Battle for Azeroth) and find Lok'rath. Purchase a special item that reveals invisible demonic runes.
  3. Step 3: Darkshore — Follow the Rune Trail
    Go to Darkshore (Cataclysm version). Use the item to reveal invisible demonic runes. Follow the trail they reveal.
  4. Step 4: Blasted Lands — Defeat Baa'l
    Travel to the Cataclysm version of Blasted Lands. Find and defeat the invisible Baa'l NPC to unlock the pet purchase.
  5. Step 5: Purchase Baa'l
    After defeating Baa'l, interact with the "Forgotten Offering" on the ground to obtain the Baa'l battle pet!

💡 Tips & Troubleshooting

  • Patch is tied to the Hearthstone Anniversary event. Verify the event is currently active before attempting.
  • The Bloody Broom is essential — without it, the yellow minimap dots will not appear.
  • Baa'l requires access to multiple expansions (WoD, BfA, Cataclysm). Ensure your character meets level and expansion requirements.
  • Both pets can be obtained solo — no group required.
  • If a part or NPC isn't showing, try /reload or relogging to reset phasing.
  • For Baa'l, make sure you are in the correct version of each zone (Cataclysm version of Darkshore and Blasted Lands). Talk to Zidormi to switch between phases.
