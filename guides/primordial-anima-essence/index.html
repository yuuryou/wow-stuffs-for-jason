Overview

The Primordial Anima Essence is a hidden transformation toy that acts like a blind box — each use randomly transforms you into various elemental creatures. The transformation lasts 20 minutes and is not canceled by entering combat. Your weapons remain visible during the transformation, creating an impressive visual effect with maximum immersion.
