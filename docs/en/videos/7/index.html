🐾 Pet #1 — Patch (Wheelchair Cat)寵物 #1 — 輪椅喵（帕奇）

A white cat in a wheelchair, a loving tribute to the Hearthstone classic character. Obtained through the anniversary hidden puzzle quest. 一隻坐著輪椅的白色貓咪，致敬《爐石戰記》經典角色。通過週年慶隱藏解密任務獲得。

Step-by-Step Guide步驟說明

  1. Go to Caverns of Time前往時光之穴
    Travel to the Caverns of Time in Tanaris.前往塔納利斯的時光之穴。
  2. Find Chromie and Get the Bloody Broom找到克羅米獲取染血的掃帚
    Inside the Caverns, find Chromie. Look for scattered parts on the ground and click them to receive the Bloody Broom.進入時光之穴後，找到 克羅米。點擊地上的散落零件來獲得 染血的掃帚。
  3. Use the Broom to Track Parts使用掃帚追蹤零件
    Equip or hold the Bloody Broom. Yellow dots will appear on your minimap indicating nearby collectible parts.裝備或持有 染血的掃帚，小地圖上會顯示黃點來指示附近可收集的零件。
  4. Collect All 5 Parts收集全部 5 個零件
    Parts may be found in areas such as: Goldshire (Elwynn Forest), Razor Hill (Durotar), Crossroads (The Barrens), Dalaran (Broken Isles), Stormwind or Orgrimmar.零件可能出現在以下區域：閃金鎮（艾爾文森林）、剃刀嶺（杜隆塔爾）、十字路口（貧瘠之地）、達拉然（破碎群島）、暴風城或奧格瑪。
  5. Return to Chromie and Assemble Patch回到克羅米處組裝帕奇
    After collecting all 5 parts, return to the Caverns of Time and speak with Chromie. Complete the assembly and you will receive the achievement "Patches the Player". Patch the pet will be mailed to you!收集完 5 個零件後，回到 時光之穴 與克羅米對話。完成組裝後會獲得成就 「全能帕奇！」，寵物帕奇會寄到你的信箱！

🐐 Pet #2 — Baa'l (Little Baa'l Wawu)寵物 #2 — 小歪比哇嗚 (Baa'l)

A secret demonic goat battle pet, returning after 5 years. Originally from Battle for Azeroth, Baa'l is one of the most challenging secret pets to obtain in WoW history. 一隻隱藏的惡魔山羊戰鬥寵物，時隔 5 年再次回歸。最早源自《決戰艾澤拉斯》，是魔獸史上最難獲得的隱藏寵物之一。

Returning in Patch 12.0712.07 版本回歸獲取方式

  1. Find the Starting NPC找到起始 NPC
    Travel to Frostfire Ridge (Warlords of Draenor) and locate the non-interactive NPC Marrow.前往 霜火峰（德拉諾之霸），找到無法直接互動的 NPC 莫羅斯。
  2. Purchase the Detection Item購買偵測道具
    Go to Vol'dun (Battle for Azeroth) and buy a special item from Lok'rath that allows you to see invisible demonic runes.前往 沃魯敦（決戰艾澤拉斯），從 洛克拉斯 處購買可以看見隱形惡魔符文的神秘道具。
  3. Find the Hidden Runes尋找隱藏符文
    Use the item in Darkshore (Cataclysm version) to reveal invisible demonic runes. Follow the rune trail.在 黑海岸（浩劫與重生版本）使用道具，來發現隱形的惡魔符文，並沿著符文線索前進。
  4. Travel to Blasted Lands前往詛咒之地
    Go to the Cataclysm version of Blasted Lands and find the invisible Baa'l NPC. Defeat it to unlock the ability to purchase the pet.前往 浩劫與重生版本的詛咒之地，找到隱形的 Baa'l NPC 並擊敗它，解鎖購買寵物的資格。
  5. Obtain Baa'l獲得小歪比哇嗚
    After defeating the invisible Baa'l, you can now purchase the Baa'l battle pet.擊敗隱形的 Baa'l 後，即可購買 小歪比哇嗚 戰鬥寵物。

💡 Tips & Troubleshooting提示與疑難排解

  • Patch is tied to the Hearthstone Anniversary event — make sure the event is active before attempting.輪椅喵 與爐石戰記週年慶活動掛鉤——開始前請確認活動仍在進行中。
  • The Bloody Broom is essential — without it, the yellow dots on your minimap will not appear.染血的掃帚 是關鍵道具——沒有它，小地圖上的黃點將不會顯示。
  • Baa'l requires access to multiple expansion zones (WoD, BfA, Cataclysm). Ensure your character meets the level requirements.小歪比哇嗚 需要前往多個資料片區域（德拉諾、決戰艾澤拉斯、浩劫與重生），確保角色等級達標。
  • Both pets can be obtained solo — no group required.兩隻寵物都可以單人完成——不需要組隊。
  • If a part isn't spawning, try relogging or /reload to reset phasing.如果零件沒出現，嘗試重新登入或 /reload 來重置鏡像。
