⚙️ Preparation前置準備

  1. Learn Fishing學習釣魚技能
    Go to Valdrakken and find Fishing Trainer Sabu to learn Dragon Isles Fishing.前往瓦德拉肯，找釣魚訓練師 Sabu 學習巨龍群島釣魚。
    /way 35.0 48.6 (Sabu - Fishing Trainer) (Sabu - 釣魚訓練師)
  2. Find NPC Lu Jia找到 NPC 陸佳
    Return to Valdrakken and locate the Raid Finder NPC Lu Jia.回到瓦德拉肯主城，找到隨機團隊 NPC 陸佳。
    /way 52.6 53.8

🌟 Phase 1 — Sleeping Moonfish x10第一階段 — 沉睡月魚 x10

  1. Enter the Scenario進入團本
    Talk to Lu Jia and select the 4th option from the bottom — "化身魚波" (Avatar of Fish Wave) to enter the raid scenario.與陸佳對話，選擇倒數第 4 個選項 「化身魚波」進入團隊副本。
  2. Fish the Pool釣取沉睡月魚
    After entering, walk straight ahead to the pool of water. Use fishing to catch 10 Sleeping Moonfish.進入後直走到正前方水池，使用釣魚技能釣取 10 條沉睡月魚。
  3. Leave the Instance離開副本
    Click your character portrait (top-left) and select "Leave Instance Group".點擊左上角人物頭像 → 離開副本。

🔥 Phase 2 — Awakened Sunfish x10第二階段 — 覺醒旭魚 x10

  1. Enter the Second Scenario進入第二個團本
    Talk to Lu Jia again and select the 3rd option from the bottom — "融火侵攻" (Molten Incursion) to enter the raid.再次與陸佳對話，選擇倒數第 3 個選項 「融火侵攻」進入團隊副本。
  2. Navigate to the Lava Ring前往岩漿處
    Wait for the NPC to finish talking, then the left door will open. Ignore trash mobs and go straight to the boss arena's circular lava ring.等 NPC 說完話左邊大門打開，忽略路上小怪，直走到 BOSS 面前的圓環岩漿處。
  3. Fish at the Lava釣取覺醒旭魚
    Use fishing to catch 10 Awakened Sunfish from the lava.在岩漿處使用釣魚技能釣取 10 條覺醒旭魚。
  4. Leave the Instance離開副本
    Click your character portrait and leave the instance.點擊人物頭像離開副本。

🎉 Phase 3 — Unlock the Appearance第三階段 — 解鎖外觀

  1. Return to the First Scenario重返第一個團本
    Talk to Lu Jia again → "化身魚波" → re-enter the first scenario.再次與陸佳對話 → 「化身魚波」 → 回到第一個團本。
  2. Throw All Fish into the Pool扔魚入池
    Walk to the pool and throw all 20 fish (10 Moonfish + 10 Sunfish) into the water.走到水池邊，將 20 條魚（10條沉睡月魚 + 10條覺醒旭魚）全部扔進水池。
  3. Obtain the Synesthesia Angler Buff獲得 Buff
    You will receive the "Synesthesia Angler" buff (3 minutes duration) in the top-right corner.右上角獲得 「通感錘釣客」 Buff（持續 3 分鐘）。
  4. Fish Up Qina釣上奇娜
    Use fishing in the pool to catch "Qina" (Soulbound item).在水池使用釣魚，釣上 「奇娜」（靈魂綁定物品）。
  5. Use the Item解鎖外觀
    Right-click Qina in your inventory → Appearance unlocked!在背包中右鍵使用「奇娜」→ 外觀解鎖！

✂️ Changing Appearance更換外觀

  1. Visit the Barber Shop前往理髮店
    Go to any Barber Shop in a major city (e.g. Valdrakken).前往任意主城的理髮店（如瓦德拉肯）。
  2. Select Aquatic Form選擇水棲形態
    Navigate to Aquatic Form → select "Dreaming Salamander".導航至水棲形態 → 選擇 「酣夢鯢鯛」。

💡 Tips小提示

  • You can fish in any form — no need to be in caster form.任何形態都可以釣魚，不需要切回人形。
  • The Synesthesia Angler buff only lasts 3 minutes — make sure to fish immediately after throwing the fish in.通感錘釣客 Buff 僅持續 3 分鐘，扔完魚後要立刻開始釣。
  • If you fail, simply leave and re-enter the scenario to try again.若失敗，只需離開副本重新進入即可重試。
  • No fishing skill level requirement for this quest — any level works.此任務無釣魚技能等級限制，任何等級均可完成。
  • This is a Druid-only appearance unlock.此為德魯伊專屬外觀解鎖。
