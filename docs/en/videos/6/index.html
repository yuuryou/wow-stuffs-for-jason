Prerequisites前置準備

  1. Translation Gear (Recommended)翻譯裝備（建議）
    Have "Translation - Mek-ghoulish" or similar translation items equipped to read hidden clues.裝備 「翻譯 - 機械俠音語」 或類似翻譯道具，以閱讀隱藏線索。
  2. Check Weekly Rotation確認週常輪換
    Some steps require specific weekly rotation. If the chest is missing, wait for the next reset.部分步驟依賴特定週常輪換。若寶箱未出現，請等待下週重置。

Step 1 — Find the Waterlogged Note步驟一 — 尋找浸水的筆記

Go to the northwest coastline of Isle of Dorn. Search for shipwrecks and underwater debris to find the Waterlogged Note. Read it with translation gear equipped to reveal the hidden clues leading to the next location.前往多恩島西北方海岸線。搜尋沉船及水下殘骸，找到 「浸水的筆記」。裝備翻譯道具後閱讀，揭示通往下一地點的隱藏線索。

Step 2 — Follow Hidden Clues步驟二 — 追蹤隱藏線索

Collect additional notes scattered across the island. Decode Morse code patterns and environmental symbols. Pay attention to references to "Deep" and "Cat" — these guide you toward an underwater cave. Use /sleep or other emotes if prompted by the clues.收集散落在島上的額外筆記。解讀摩斯密碼及環境符號。注意「深處」與「貓」的提示 — 這些將引導你前往水下洞穴。必要時依線索使用 /sleep 或其他表情動作。

Step 3 — Obtain Placid Deep Cat步驟三 — 獲取泰然深潛貓

Navigate the hidden underwater cave. You may need a buff that reveals invisible paths. In the deepest chamber, find the hidden treasure chest and open it to receive the Placid Deep Cat pet.探索隱藏水下洞穴。你可能需要揭示隱形路徑的增益效果。在最深處的密室，找到隱藏寶箱，打開即可獲得 泰然深潛貓 寵物。
