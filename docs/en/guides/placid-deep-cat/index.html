Prerequisites前置準備

  1. Arrive at Isle of Dorn抵達多恩島
    Travel to the Isle of Dorn zone. This is the starting area for the secret puzzle.前往多恩島區域，此為解密謎題的起始地點。
  2. Translation Gear (Recommended)翻譯裝備（建議）
    It is highly recommended to have translation gear such as "Translation - Mek-ghoulish" or similar items that allow you to read cryptic notes scattered across the island.強烈建議準備翻譯裝備，例如 「翻譯 - 機械俠音語」 或類似可閱讀島上加密筆記的道具。
  3. Check Weekly Rotation確認週常輪換
    Some decryption steps may be tied to weekly rotation. If you cannot find the chest, you may need to wait for the next weekly reset.部分解密步驟可能涉及週常輪換。若找不到解密寶箱，可能需要等待下週重置後再嘗試。

Step 1 — Find the Waterlogged Note步驟一 — 尋找浸水的筆記

  1. Search the Northwest Coast搜尋西北方海岸
    Head to the northwest coastline of Isle of Dorn. Look for shipwrecks or underwater areas near the shore.前往多恩島西北方海岸線。在近海的沉船或水下區域尋找。
  2. Locate the Waterlogged Note找到浸水的筆記
    Find the Waterlogged Note item. It may spawn in multiple locations near the water — check wreckage debris thoroughly.找到 「浸水的筆記」 物品。它可能在水邊多個位置刷新 — 仔細檢查沉船殘骸。
  3. Read the Note閱讀筆記
    Right-click the note to read it. If the text appears as gibberish, equip your translation gear and try again — the hidden message will be revealed.右鍵點擊筆記閱讀。如果文字顯示為亂碼，裝備翻譯道具後再試 — 隱藏訊息將會顯現。
Tip:提示： If the Waterlogged Note is not visible, try switching shards or relogging. Some players report the note may be phased.若浸水的筆記沒有出現，嘗試切換位面或重新登入。部分玩家反映該筆記可能存在鏡像問題。

Step 2 — Follow the Hidden Clues步驟二 — 追蹤隱藏線索

  1. Collect Scattered Notes收集散落的筆記
    The Waterlogged Note will give you clues to find additional notes scattered across Isle of Dorn. Follow the hints carefully — some notes are hidden under environmental objects.浸水的筆記會指引你尋找散落在多恩島各處的其他筆記。仔細跟隨提示 — 部分筆記隱藏在環境物件下。
  2. Decode Morse Code / Symbols解讀摩斯密碼 / 符號
    Some clues involve Morse code patterns or symbols in the environment. Pay attention to unusual markings on rocks, trees, or structures. Use /sleep or other emotes if prompted.部分線索涉及環境中的摩斯密碼或符號。注意岩石、樹木或建築上的異常標記。必要時使用 /sleep 或其他表情動作觸發下一步。
  3. Look for "Deep" and "Cat" References尋找「深處」與「貓」的提示
    The puzzle themes revolve around "Deep" and "Cat" — expect to head toward underwater caves or deep underground areas on the island.謎題主題圍繞「深處」與「貓」— 預期需要前往島上的水下洞穴或深層區域。

Step 3 — Obtain the Placid Deep Cat步驟三 — 獲取泰然深潛貓

  1. Navigate the Underwater Cave進入水下洞穴
    After solving the clues, make your way to a hidden underwater cave on Isle of Dorn. You may need a temporary buff that reveals invisible paths.解開所有線索後，前往多恩島的隱藏水下洞穴。你可能需要一個揭示隱形路徑的暫時增益效果。
  2. Find the Hidden Chest尋找隱藏寶箱
    In the deepest part of the cave, locate a hidden treasure chest or interact with a concealed NPC.在洞穴最深處，找到隱藏的寶箱或與隱形 NPC 互動。
  3. Loot the Pet拾取寵物
    Open the chest to receive the Placid Deep Cat pet. The item will be automatically added to your pet collection.打開寶箱獲得 泰然深潛貓 寵物。物品將自動加入你的寵物收藏。

Tips & Troubleshooting提示與疑難排解

  • Chest not appearing? Some decryption steps may be tied to weekly rotation. If the chest is missing, try again after the next weekly reset.找不到寶箱？部分解密步驟可能與週常輪換有關。若寶箱未出現，請在下週重置後再試。
  • Can't read the notes? Make sure you have equipped translation gear (such as Translation - Mek-ghoulish). Without it, the notes will appear as scrambled text.無法閱讀筆記？確保已裝備翻譯道具（如 翻譯 - 機械俠音語）。沒有翻譯道具，筆記內容將顯示為亂碼。
  • This pet is a reskin of the Fire Cat pet from the Mists of Pandaria era — same model, different color scheme.此寵物為換皮版本，原型為潘達利亞之謎時期的火貓寵物 — 相同模型，不同配色。
  • No combat required — this is purely a puzzle/exploration activity.無需戰鬥 — 此為純粹的解謎探索活動。
  • Switching shards can sometimes help if a note or chest is missing in your current phase.切換位面有時可解決筆記或寶箱在當前鏡像中不顯示的問題。
