🐾 Pet #1 — Patch (Wheelchair Cat)寵物 #1 — 輪椅喵（帕奇）

A white cat in a wheelchair, a loving tribute to the classic Hearthstone character "Patches". This pet is obtained through the Hearthstone Anniversary hidden puzzle quest, earning you the achievement "Patches the Player" (全能帕奇！). 一隻坐著輪椅的白色貓咪，溫馨致敬《爐石戰記》經典角色「帕奇」。通過爐石週年慶隱藏解密任務獲得，解鎖成就 「全能帕奇！」。

How to Obtain獲取方式

  1. Travel to Caverns of Time前往時光之穴
    Go to the Caverns of Time in Tanaris and find Chromie.前往塔納利斯的時光之穴，找到 克羅米。
  2. Obtain the Bloody Broom獲取染血的掃帚
    Look for scattered parts on the ground near Chromie and click them. You will receive the Bloody Broom (染血的掃帚).在克羅米附近尋找散落的零件並點擊，獲得 染血的掃帚。
  3. Track Parts with the Broom使用掃帚追蹤零件
    Equip or hold the Bloody Broom — yellow dots will appear on your minimap indicating nearby collectible parts.裝備或持有染血的掃帚，小地圖上會顯示黃點，指示附近可收集的零件位置。
  4. Collect All 5 Parts收集全部 5 個零件
    The 5 parts are scattered across Azeroth. Common locations include:5 個零件散布在艾澤拉斯各處，常見位置包括：
    • Goldshire, Elwynn Forest閃金鎮，艾爾文森林
    • Razor Hill, Durotar剃刀嶺，杜隆塔爾
    • Crossroads, The Barrens十字路口，貧瘠之地
    • Dalaran, Broken Isles達拉然，破碎群島
    • Stormwind City / Orgrimmar暴風城 / 奧格瑪
  5. Assemble Patch and Claim Your Pet組裝帕奇並領取寵物
    Return to Chromie at the Caverns of Time. Complete the assembly quest and earn the achievement "Patches the Player". Patch will be mailed to you!回到時光之穴找克羅米，完成組裝任務後獲得成就 「全能帕奇！」，帕奇會寄到你的信箱！

🐐 Pet #2 — Baa'l (Little Baa'l Wawu)寵物 #2 — 小歪比哇嗚 (Baa'l)

Baa'l is a secret demonic goat battle pet that first appeared in Battle for Azeroth. After a 5-year wait, Baa'l returns in Patch 12.07 with a new way to obtain this elusive companion. Originally one of the most complex secret hunts in WoW history, the 12.07 version streamlines some steps while preserving the mystery. 小歪比哇嗚 (Baa'l) 是一隻隱藏的惡魔山羊戰鬥寵物，最早在《決戰艾澤拉斯》亮相。經過 5 年的等待，Baa'l 在 12.07 版本回歸，並提供新的獲取途徑。原本是魔獸史上最複雜的隱藏任務之一，12.07 版本簡化了部分步驟，同時保留了神秘感。

How to Obtain (12.07 Version)獲取方式（12.07 版本）

  1. Step 1: Frostfire Ridge — Find Marrow步驟 1：霜火峰 — 尋找莫羅斯
    Travel to Frostfire Ridge (Warlords of Draenor) and locate the non-interactive NPC Marrow near Thunderlord Stronghold.前往霜火峰（德拉諾之霸），在雷霆王氏族要塞附近找到無法直接互動的 NPC 莫羅斯。
  2. Step 2: Vol'dun — Buy the Rune Detector步驟 2：沃魯敦 — 購買符文偵測器
    Travel to Vol'dun (Battle for Azeroth) and find Lok'rath. Purchase a special item that reveals invisible demonic runes.前往沃魯敦（決戰艾澤拉斯），尋找 洛克拉斯 並購買可看見隱形惡魔符文的特殊道具。
  3. Step 3: Darkshore — Follow the Rune Trail步驟 3：黑海岸 — 追蹤符文線索
    Go to Darkshore (Cataclysm version). Use the item to reveal invisible demonic runes. Follow the trail they reveal.前往黑海岸（浩劫與重生版本），使用道具顯示隱形的惡魔符文，並沿著符文所示的線索前進。
  4. Step 4: Blasted Lands — Defeat Baa'l步驟 4：詛咒之地 — 擊敗 Baa'l
    Travel to the Cataclysm version of Blasted Lands. Find and defeat the invisible Baa'l NPC to unlock the pet purchase.前往浩劫與重生版本的詛咒之地，找到並擊敗隱形的 Baa'l NPC 以解鎖寵物購買資格。
  5. Step 5: Purchase Baa'l步驟 5：購買小歪比哇嗚
    After defeating Baa'l, interact with the "Forgotten Offering" on the ground to obtain the Baa'l battle pet!擊敗 Baa'l 後，與地上的「被遺忘的祭品」互動，即可獲得 小歪比哇嗚 戰鬥寵物！

💡 Tips & Troubleshooting提示與疑難排解

  • Patch is tied to the Hearthstone Anniversary event. Verify the event is currently active before attempting.輪椅喵 與爐石戰記週年慶活動掛鉤，開始前請先確認活動仍在進行中。
  • The Bloody Broom is essential — without it, the yellow minimap dots will not appear.染血的掃帚是必要道具——沒有它，小地圖上的黃點將不會出現。
  • Baa'l requires access to multiple expansions (WoD, BfA, Cataclysm). Ensure your character meets level and expansion requirements.小歪比哇嗚 需要跨越多個資料片（德拉諾、決戰艾澤拉斯、浩劫與重生），請確保角色等級與資料片進度達標。
  • Both pets can be obtained solo — no group required.兩隻寵物都可單人完成——無需組隊。
  • If a part or NPC isn't showing, try /reload or relogging to reset phasing.如果零件或 NPC 沒出現，嘗試 /reload 或重新登入來重置鏡像。
  • For Baa'l, make sure you are in the correct version of each zone (Cataclysm version of Darkshore and Blasted Lands). Talk to Zidormi to switch between phases.關於 Baa'l，請確保身處正確的區域版本（浩劫與重生版的黑海岸和詛咒之地）。可與希爾多米對話來切換鏡像。
